# Elevation

**The rule: a card gets a border, never a shadow. Only a floating layer gets one.**

There are two shadows and a `none`, and `none` is the one almost everything uses. The standing note
on the app this replaces is that its panel shadow reads as "something thick", and that is what
happens when a shadow is used to separate a card from a page: the page and the card are on the same
plane, so the shadow is describing a depth that is not there. An edge does the job at 1px and costs
the layout nothing.

A shadow is for a layer that genuinely floats, which means one that is temporary and covers content
the reader was just looking at: a popover, a dropdown, a tooltip, a modal. There the shadow is not
decoration, it is the only cue that the thing underneath is still there.

Both shadows compose their colour from the `shadow-color` role, which is why dark mode can drop them
to zero without touching a component: on a near-black ground a shadow is invisible, so the surface
ladder carries depth instead. That is also why the ladder steps wider in dark than it needs to in
light.
