# Space and shape

## Space

A 4pt base with two half steps below it, because a dense interface needs 2px and 6px more than it
needs 64px. Every bar below is drawn at its real width, so the gap between adjacent steps is visible
rather than asserted: `space-1` through `space-4` are the inside of a control, `space-5` and
`space-6` are the inside of a card, and everything above `space-8` is between blocks.

`space-px` exists so a 1px gap in a flex column is a token rather than a literal, which is what keeps
the divided lists in the filter popovers from drifting to 2px one at a time.

## Radius

Radius encodes size, not taste: the smaller the thing, the tighter the corner, so a chip inside a
card never looks rounder than the card holding it.

## Border width

<BorderWidths />

There is no 2px rule in this system, and the second token exists to say so out loud. `lineWidthBold`
is handed to Ant Design as 1 for the same reason: its default is a 2px focus and active edge, and a
hairline system has to override that once, centrally, rather than per component. Emphasis comes from
the border colour stepping up the neutral ramp, `border-subtle` to `border-default` to
`border-strong`, never from the line getting thicker.
